## 中文

Minecraft 1.7.10 / GTNH，支持 **GTNH 2.9.0-RC1 及以上版本**。

### ME 存储与流向

- 修复 ME 终端的物质流向重复计数。合成产物进入 CPU 时不记为入库，产物实际写入网络存储时才计数。该修复也覆盖经 ME 存储总线转发给子网 CPU 的产物。
- 改进旧版无限存储迁移的回执校验与保存顺序，保留接口没有成功转移的物资。
- 在客户端主线程更新存储界面快照，撤回样板时重新定位当前网络中的原槽位。

### 合成与计算子系统

- 合成确认校验计划和样板目录版本，修复 CPU 选择与合并提示。计划树按实际样板执行次数分摊共享批次，按物品栏快捷键可关闭合成预览。
- 修复 CPU 恢复时的发布顺序、批次归属与派发结算，保留精确库存和合成报告数量。
- 紧凑计算子系统由通讯接口提供外部 ME 接线。已成型集群复用边界及镜像方向复查结构。
- 合成供应器按需遍历，空闲工人使用快照派发，完成或取消的订单按索引处理工人。精确订单限制每 Tick 的补料检查量，并在材料到货时提前重试。

### 机器与兼容

- 按 GTNR 总成选定的配方池匹配样板上传目标，修复手动上传分组计数，并保留向 GTSWN 装配矩阵交料时的样板槽位。
- 工作站按相同分配结果匹配和扣除原料，按实际扣电推进加工。更新加载依赖声明及部分整合兼容逻辑。

## English

Minecraft 1.7.10 / GTNH. Supports **GTNH 2.9.0-RC1 and later**.

### ME storage and flow tracking

- Fixed double counting in ME terminal flow tracking. Crafting output is counted when it enters network storage. This applies to both local CPUs and subnet CPUs reached through ME Storage Buses.
- Improved migration receipts and save ordering for legacy infinite storage. Interfaces retain resources they could not transfer.
- Apply storage snapshots on the client thread and resolve the original slot on the current grid when undoing a pattern upload.

### Crafting and computation

- Validate plan and pattern catalog revisions at confirmation, and fix CPU selection and merge prompts. The plan tree counts shared batches once. The inventory key closes the crafting preview.
- Fixed CPU publication during recovery, batch ownership checks, and dispatch accounting. Preserve exact quantities in storage and crafting reports.
- Compact computation subsystems expose their external ME connection through the communication interface. Formed clusters reuse their bounds and orientation during structure checks.
- Scan providers on demand, reuse snapshots of idle workers, and look up workers by order on completion or cancellation. Bound material refill checks per tick and retry sooner when stock arrives.

### Machines and compatibility

- Match GTNR assembly pattern uploads against the selected recipe pool, fix manual upload target counts, and preserve pattern input slots when dispatching to GTSWN assembly matrices.
- Workstations use the same allocation for recipe matching and ingredient consumption. Processing advances according to energy actually consumed. Updated mod dependency declarations and compatibility handling.
