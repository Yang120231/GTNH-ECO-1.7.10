# Obtaining Energized Crystals

<ItemLink id="neoecoae:energized_crystal" /> is a basic material for ECO components.

## The First Crystal

With GregTech installed, process **4 Charged Certus Quartz Crystals** in an **MV Arc Furnace** for **10 seconds** to obtain **1 Energized Crystal**. Check NEI for the machine recipe.

## Producing More Crystals

Grind Energized Crystals into <ItemLink id="neoecoae:energized_crystal_dust" />, then process the dust with Charged Certus Quartz Crystals and water to produce more crystals. With GregTech installed, use a Macerator and Autoclave; consult NEI for quantities, duration and voltage.

The Integrated Working Station also provides an Energized Crystal recipe. Crystals and crystal blocks can be converted through crafting.
